# Market and competition
Research register: [ASUG/SAP integration survey](https://www.sap.com/documents/2026/02/ec1b4a80-3f7f-0010-bca6-c68f7e60039b.html), [SAP community on integration](https://www.reddit.com/r/SAP/comments/1ou7ijd/whats_the_one_sap_integration_or_workflow_that/), [rework discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/SAP/comments/1t3dmif/we_had_to_bring_in_a_second_sap_consultant_after/), [AMS procurement](https://govtribe.com/file/government-file/250521-sap-ams-rfp-2025-final-dot-pdf). Survey is not census; anecdotes are not prevalence; procurement is buying signal not AI-provider demand. Revalidate before publication.
| Competitor | Research lead | Differentiation to test |
|---|---|---|
| SAP | Joule, Signavio, LeanIX, Cloud ALM, agent gateway | Cross-vendor and customer-owned change |
| Deloitte | RACE configuration automation | Smaller incremental outcomes |
| Accenture | GenWizard, AI factory delivery | Outcome economics and portability |
| Capgemini | AutoRoot.AI | Cross-system diagnosis |
| [Sookti AI](https://sookti.ai/) | AI-native SAP delivery; [logistics case](https://sookti.ai/case-studies/global-mining-natural-resources) | Continuous evolution, partner channel, independent evidence |
| Pillir | ERP execution layer | Delivery and learning, not only runtime |
Vendor claims unverified; absence of public evidence is not absence of capability. Interview CIOs, business owners, ISVs, boutiques and large SIs. Do not assume integration is the only beachhead.
