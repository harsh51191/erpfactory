# Business thesis
Sell continuous enterprise evolution, not cheaper code. SAP first; configuration, custom development, integration, migration, optimization, assurance, application support and process improvement are all in scope. Test buyer willingness to pay at incumbent price parity for faster value, capacity, quality, reliable support and knowledge ownership. Measure critical-path days saved, backlog unlocked, rework, MTTR, accepted outcomes per human hour and total cost. Offers: readiness assessment, outcome-based delivery, managed reliability, continuous evolution. Falsify if only price matters, access is infeasible or safe support economics fail.
