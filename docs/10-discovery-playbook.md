# Customer discovery playbook
Do not lead with AI or price. Interview actual buyers, operators and partners separately. Ask for the last real change or failure and walk through the timeline, artifacts, approvals, waiting and execution. Ask for deferred changes and quantified consequences. Validate willingness to pay at price parity, decision owner, procurement/access constraints, support ownership. Seek sanitized historical artifacts and authorized test environment only after agreement.
## 45 minutes
5 context; 15 last actual change (requirements, access, functional design, transport, testing, release); 10 last incident and deferred backlog; 10 economics, trust, constraints and buying; 5 confirm findings and next step. Record quotes privately with consent; never publish names or customer evidence.
## Interview archetypes
ISV delivery leader: go-live critical path, coordination, concurrent implementations, revenue delay. Boutique SAP partner: actual execution, custom code, sandbox, tests, support, partner capacity. Large SI executive: operating model, economics, procurement, risk and existing automation. ERP owner: business outcomes, backlog, release control, vendor lock-in and renewal.
## Evidence template
Last change, scope, timestamps, wait vs execution, artifacts, rework, outcome, owner, source, counterexample, confidence. Ask what would disprove the proposed benefit. End with an optional paid, bounded pilot, not a sales pitch.
