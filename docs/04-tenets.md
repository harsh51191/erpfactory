# Tenets and gates
Canonical list: [ERP tenets](../rules/erp-tenets.md). Gates: readiness, access, scope/placement, impact, implementation, independent proof, release, operational acceptance. Never advance based on missing evidence. Proof levels 1 written, 2 checked, 3 exercised, 4 shown, 5 accepted, 6 shipped, 7 proven; always bind to environment and revision.
