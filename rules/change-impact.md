# Change impact and placement
Prefer existing standard functionality, then supported configuration, released APIs and extensions, then supported classic mechanisms where appropriate. Do not modify standard core or directly write standard business tables. Put enterprise-wide invariants in the owning transactional system; optimization in its owning application; transport transformations in middleware. Assess technical, functional, organizational, integration, operational, compliance and capacity effects. Traverse dependency graph, add mandatory baseline regressions; unknown/dynamic links require review. No graph is proof of completeness.
